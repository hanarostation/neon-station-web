Neon Station Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 4, 2026 (announced November 27, 2026) · Operator: Datastation (the “Operator”) · 한국어

The Operator provides the mobile game “Neon Station” (the “Game”) in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws, and processes users’ personal information as described below. The Game starts with an anonymous account without sign-up, and you may choose to link a Google or Apple account. If you do not link an account, we do not collect contact information such as your name, phone number or email address.

We use the analytics tool PostHog for usage statistics and error analysis, and these records are linked to the account identifier above. Payment method information such as card numbers is handled by Google and Apple and is never received by the Operator. The Game does not use location permission (GPS), contacts, photos or the microphone.

1-1. Ads and personalized ads

  • The Game shows only rewarded ads from Google AdMob, and an ad plays only when you tap a button such as “Watch ad for 3x”. There are no banner or interstitial ads. If you buy the permanent ad removal, you receive the rewards without ads.
  • Android: personalized ads may be shown. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device’s Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads) to stop personalized ads.
  • iOS: we do not ask for tracking permission (ATT) and request non-personalized ads only, without the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see an ad consent form on first launch.
  • How Google uses information from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2. Retention and deletion

  • Account and server records (including linked Google/Apple account information, progress backups, recovery code hashes and currency balances): kept until you delete your account, and deleted without delay when you request deletion. When you make a new recovery code, the hash of the previous code is deleted at once.
  • Recovery code entry records: deleted automatically 1 day after entry.
  • Currency change records (changes and sources): deleted automatically 90 days after they are recorded.
  • Native crash reports (Sentry): kept for up to 90 days from collection, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: kept for 5 years as required by the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (records of payment and supply of goods). When you delete your account, we unlink the purchase records from it and keep only those records until the period ends.
  • Chat messages: only the latest 500 messages are kept; older messages are deleted automatically.
  • Reports: deleted within 6 months after the report is handled (also deleted when the account is deleted).
  • App usage and error records (analytics tool): kept for 1 year from collection, then deleted. When you request account deletion, records linked to that account identifier are deleted as well.
  • Deletion is carried out so that the data cannot be recovered from the database.

5. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • In the Game, go to Profile › Info › Delete account & data to immediately delete your server account and records (including linked account information, progress backups, recovery codes and currency records) and the progress on your device. Purchase records are kept separately from the account for the legal period described in section 2.
  • If you have already removed the app, email 0001jmp@gmail.com with the subject “Neon Station account deletion request” and the nickname you used in the Game. We will delete your data within 10 days and let you know.
  • Send requests to access, correct or suspend processing of your information to the contact below. We will reply within 10 days.

6. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 14. If we learn that a user is a child under 14, we will delete that information.

7. Security measures

We encrypt data in transit (HTTPS), separate database access rights (row-level security, with administrator keys kept only on the server), and manage access logs.
