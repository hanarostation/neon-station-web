Neon Station Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026 · Operator: Datastation (the “Operator”) · 한국어

The Operator provides the mobile game “Neon Station” (the “Game”) in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws, and processes users’ personal information as described below. The Game is played with an anonymous account, without sign-up or login, and does not collect contact information such as your name, phone number or email address.

Most game progress (currencies, inventory, etc.) is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Operator. We use the analytics tool PostHog for usage statistics and error analysis, and these records are linked to the anonymous account identifier above. This version does not use advertising SDKs, location permission (GPS), contacts, photos or the microphone, and does not track you for targeted advertising or combine your data with data from other companies.

2. Retention and deletion

  • Account and server records: kept until you delete your account, and deleted without delay when you request deletion.
  • Chat messages: only the latest 500 messages are kept; older messages are deleted automatically.
  • Reports: deleted within 6 months after the report is handled (also deleted when the account is deleted).
  • App usage and error records (analytics tool): kept for 1 year from collection, then deleted. When you request account deletion, records linked to that account identifier are deleted as well.
  • Deletion is carried out so that the data cannot be recovered from the database.

5. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • In the Game, go to Profile › Info › Delete account & data to immediately delete your server account and records and the progress on your device.
  • If you have already removed the app, email 0001jmp@gmail.com with the subject “Neon Station account deletion request” and the nickname you used in the Game. We will delete your data within 10 days and let you know.
  • Send requests to access, correct or suspend processing of your information to the contact below. We will reply within 10 days.

6. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 14. If we learn that a user is a child under 14, we will delete that information.

7. Security measures

We encrypt data in transit (HTTPS), separate database access rights (row-level security, with administrator keys kept only on the server), and manage access logs.
