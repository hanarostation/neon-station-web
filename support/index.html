FAQ

I saw an offensive chat message.
Tap the sender's name in chat to open their profile, then tap Report or Block. Reported messages are reviewed by the operator.

How do I delete my account and data?
In the app, open Profile and use the account deletion menu. Details: Account & data deletion

Where can I see the drop odds?
All grade odds and pity (guaranteed drop) rules are shown in the in-game odds information.
