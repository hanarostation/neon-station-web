Why a data company made a game

There is something we always say in class: if you pick tools before defining the problem, the project wobbles. Neon Station began as a way to live by that ourselves. With a real game development process as the map, each stage had set outputs, and the empty roles were shared with AI. Balance is checked with simulations, decisions are kept in a log, and the documents are updated every morning.

That process has become teaching material, carried on in the Brunch series and in class case studies.
